# Skills und Fertigkeiten

Skills beginnen laut DOCX bei 0. Im Abenteuer koennen sie mit Skillpunkten gelernt und gesteigert werden. Gegenstands- und Effektboni werden neben dem Skill notiert und veraendern die Erfolge beim Wurf. Ein Startbudget an Skillpunkten ist nicht angegeben.

## Skills
**Athletics**  
        _“You climbed the fence instead of finding the gate because thinking is cardio for nerds.”_ Domains: Climbing, Running, Swimming, Jumping, Acrobatics, Physical Endurance, Stamina, Balance
    
**Channel**  
        _“A direct mental connection to incomprehensible cosmic forces. What could possibly go wrong.”_ Domains: Psionics, Mental Projection, Telepathy, Energy Channeling, Astral Perception, Anomalies
    
**Control**  
        _“If there’s a blinking light and a joystick, you will absolutely press the wrong button with confidence.”_  
        Domains: Drone Control, Vehicle Handling, Turret Operation, Machine Control, Remote Systems
    
**Defend**  
        _“Not necessarily winning. Mostly just surviving slightly longer than the other idiot.”_  
        Domains: Blocking, Dodging, Taking Cover, Shield Techniques, Defensive Stances, Damage Resistance
    
**Influence**  
        _“Some call it charisma. Others call it a red flag.”_  
        Domains: Leadership, Negotiation, Intimidation, Manipulation, Diplomacy, Social Control, Motivation
    
**Investigate**  
        _“You treat every conversation like cross-examination and somehow still wonder why people avoid you.”_ Domains: Investigation, Crime Scene Analysis, Pattern Recognition, Interrogation, Observation, Forensics
    
**Medicine**  
        _“People trust you with surgery despite the fact you haven’t slept in 36 hours.”_ Domains: First Aid, Surgery, Caretaking, Diagnostics, Pharmacology, Anatomy, Medical Knowledge
    
**Shoot**  
        _“Your conflict resolution strategy travels faster than sound.”_ Domains: Pistols, Rifles, Sniper Weapons, Heavy Weapons, Target Acquisition, Ballistics
    
**Stealth**  
        _“You disappear so effectively people forget to include you in plans. Exactly how every edgelord likes it.”_ Domains: Sneaking, Hiding, Camouflage, Silent Movement, Shadowing, Infiltration
    
**Strike**  
        _“Anger management failed. Here we are.”_  
        Domains: Melee Weapons, Martial Arts, Unarmed Combat, Aggressive Tactics, Close Combat
    
**Streetwise**  
        _“You know which alley sells illegal weapons and which one sells surprisingly good noodles.”_ Domains: Black Markets, Urban Networks, Gangs, Rumors, Slums, Smuggling, Street Culture
    
**Survival**  
        _“Nature wants you dead. You took that personally.”_  
        Domains: Hunting, Navigation, Camp Building, Wilderness Knowledge, Foraging, Weather Reading, Tracking
    
**Piloting**  
        _“Parallel parking, but the wall is an asteroid field and everyone dies if you fail.”_  
        Domains: Navigation, Spaceflight, Vehicle Operation, Astrogation, Flight Maneuvers, Travel Planning
    
**Thievery**  
        _“Security systems are merely strongly worded suggestions.”_  
        Domains: Lockpicking, Pickpocketing, Burglary, Trap Disarming, Restricted Access, Smuggling
    
**Arts**  
        _“Finally, a skill for emotionally damaged people with expensive hobbies.”_  
        Domains: Music, Dance, Painting, Acting, Literature, Performance, Artistic Expression
    
**Culture**  
        _“Explaining why civilizations collapse since civilization was invented.”_  
        Domains: Politics, Economics, Law, History, Society, Religion, Ideologies, Diplomacy
    
**Physical Science**  
        _“Turning ‘what if?’ into explosions since forever.”_  
        Domains: Physics, Chemistry, Material Science, Energy Systems, Thermodynamics, Reactions, Laboratory Work
    
**Life Science**  
        _“Studying life in great detail while forgetting to drink water yourself.”_  
        Domains: Biology, Flora, Fauna, Evolution, Genetics, Ecology, Environmental Science
    
**Mysteries**  
        _“Reality stopped making sense around you several sessions ago.”_ Domains: Psionic Phenomena, Paranormal Events, Madan, Cosmic Anomalies, Unexplained Forces
    
**Computing**  
        _“You can hack military infrastructure but still lose passwords immediately.”_ Domains: Programming, Networking, Hacking, Software Development, Data Analysis, Cybersecurity
    
**Electronics**  
        _“Every electrician claims the smell of burnt circuits is ‘normal.’”_  
        Domains: Circuits, Sensors, Power Systems, Wiring, Electronic Repair, Energy Distribution
    
**Engineering**  
        _“Half genius, half duct tape, somehow legally employed.”_  
        Domains: Construction, Repairs, Mechanical Engineering, Structural Systems, Ship Systems, Infrastructure, Improvised Technology
